Research

Research & Computational Thinking

Mathematics and computational statistics sit at the core of how Rabiatu approaches problems: providing the structure to ask precise questions and the methods to answer them.

Multilevel Computational Modelling

The MSc research focuses on modelling students' academic performance across public secondary schools. Because students are grouped within schools, and schools sit within wider regional contexts, the work uses multilevel modelling, a statistical approach designed for data that is naturally nested.

Rather than treating every student as an isolated data point, multilevel models recognise that context matters. This makes it possible to explore how individual and group-level factors relate to academic performance in a way that is more faithful to the real structure of the data.
